# LucidGrasp Acceptable Use Policy

Effective: October 2026

LucidGrasp indexes folders on your own machine and finds visually similar
images. It can search whatever folders you point it at, up to a whole
filesystem. This policy states the limits on that freedom.

## 1. Lawful use only

You must use the Software in compliance with all applicable laws, including:

- copyright and other intellectual property laws,
- data protection and privacy laws,
- computer misuse, unauthorized access, and cybercrime laws of your
  jurisdiction (for example the US Computer Fraud and Abuse Act and equivalent
  laws elsewhere), and
- export, import, and sanctions laws that apply to you.

## 2. Authorized systems only

Index only files and systems you own or are authorized by the owner to
access. Do not use the Software against systems, networks, or data you have no
right to read.

## 3. What you may not do

You may not use the Software to:

- plan, facilitate, or commit any unlawful act,
- infringe another person's copyright or other rights,
- surveil, track, or profile individuals without lawful basis,
- defeat, bypass, or evade security or access controls you are not authorized
  to use,
- deliver or distribute malware, spyware, or other harmful software, or
- misrepresent the Software, its author, or its affiliation — for example, by
  reselling LucidGrasp, claiming endorsement that does not exist, or using the
  project name to imply provenance that is not genuine.

## 4. Redistribution

The source code and binaries may be redistributed under the [MIT License](LICENSE).
Redistributors must keep the license text and copyright notice, mark their
modifications clearly, and must not represent modified copies as the official
project.

## 5. Enforcement

If you become aware of a violation of this policy, or a vulnerability that
could enable one, report it: security matters go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md),
everything else to [adityaraj1234@duck.com](mailto:adityaraj1234@duck.com).

Violations may result in a request to stop, and the project reserves the right
